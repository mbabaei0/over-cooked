# Spike note: Space Station Galley, zero-g cooking

**Question tested:** Is cooking still fun, and funny, when gravity cuts out and loose ingredients float away?

**Result:** YES. The person played it and said: "Yes, it's fun".

**What was built:** one galley seen from a tilted 2.5D camera, 1–4 local players (keyboard and gamepads), a chop → pot → cook → bowl → serve recipe with three soups, and a 3-minute shift scored with 1–3 stars and no way to fail. Gravity cuts out at 55s, then every 32–44s for 7s. Everything not held floats away, pots stop cooking, and cooks drift and bounce; you can catch items mid-air or throw them. Tuning values are in `config.js`.

**What to do next:** put zero-g on the game's build order as the signature hook, then write the full brief (`/brainstorm` → "write it down", or `/prototype` for a full slice).
